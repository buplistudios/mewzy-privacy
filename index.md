Mewzy Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 8, 2026

Mewzy is a mobile game made by Bupli Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Game progress
Your cats, items, currencies and settings are saved on your device.

Google Play Games sign-in
If you sign in with Google Play Games, we receive your Play Games player ID and display name. We use them to save your progress and to show your name to other players.

Online lobby, chat and trades
When you use online features, your player name, chat messages and trades are sent to our game server so other players can see them and trades can be completed.

Purchases
All payments are handled by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation (such as the order ID and the item bought) so we can deliver your item and handle refunds. We never see or store your card or bank details.

Ads
Mewzy shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads are never forced. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show and measure ads. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Crash and usage data
We may collect basic information about how the game is used and about crashes, so we can fix bugs and improve the game.

2. How we use information
- To run the game and save your progress
- To run the lobby, chat and trades
- To deliver purchases and handle refunds
- To show optional ads
- To fix bugs and improve the game
- To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse

3. Sharing
We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with services that help run the game: Google (Play Games, Play Billing and AdMob) and our server hosting provider. We may also share information if the law requires it.

4. Children
Mewzy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Chat safety
Chat messages may be checked and removed if they break our rules. Players who abuse chat or trading may be blocked.

6. Keeping and deleting your data
Game progress saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. To delete your online data (player name, chat messages and trade history), email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

7. Your choices
You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

8. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
